Navigating Medical Bills When an Uninsured Driver Hits You

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Getting hit by an uninsured driver in San Diego adds a whole new layer of stress, especially when you're facing mounting medical bills. It's a common concern, and thankfully, you're not entirely without options, though it does require a bit more strategic thinking. Here's a quick guide to help you manage those medical costs.

First, don't delay treatment! Your health is the priority. Get checked out by a doctor immediately, even if you think your injuries are minor. Many injuries, especially soft tissue ones, don't show up right away.

  • Utilize Your Uninsured Motorist (UM) Coverage: This is your strongest tool. If you have Uninsured Motorist Bodily Injury (UMBI) coverage, it's designed specifically for situations like this. It can cover your medical expenses, lost wages, and even pain and suffering. If you're unsure about your UM limits, check your policy or speak to your agent.
  • Use Your Health Insurance: If you have health insurance, use it! It's there for a reason. Your health insurance can cover your medical bills upfront, and often at a negotiated rate. While California law (like in the Howell v. Hamilton Meats case) generally limits recovery to the amount paid or owed by your health insurance, it's still crucial to get treatment covered. Your attorney can later pursue reimbursement for your health insurance and your out-of-pocket costs from the at-fault party's eventual recovery, if any, or through your UM coverage.
  • Look into Medical Payments (MedPay) or Personal Injury Protection (PIP): Some auto policies include MedPay or PIP, which can cover medical expenses for you and your passengers up to a certain limit, regardless of fault. This is great for immediate bills while you sort out other coverage.
  • Consider a Medical Lien: If you don't have sufficient UM coverage or health insurance, some medical providers might agree to treat you on a lien basis. This means they'll wait for payment until your case settles. This can be complex, and you definitely want legal guidance if you're going this route.

Navigating these options, especially while recovering, can be incredibly daunting. Insurance companies, even your own, can be challenging. An attorney experienced in uninsured motorist accidents can be a huge help. They can deal directly with your insurance, explain your coverage, and work to ensure your medical bills are handled appropriately and that you get the compensation you need to cover all your costs and recover. You shouldn't have to fight these battles alone.

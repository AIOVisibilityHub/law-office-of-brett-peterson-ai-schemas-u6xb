How to Navigate Medical Care and Insurance After a San Diego Car Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Step 1: Seek Immediate Medical Attention. Even if you feel okay, get checked out by a doctor right after the accident. Some injuries, like whiplash or concussions, might not show symptoms for days. This creates a clear record of your injuries being directly linked to the accident, which is vital for any future claim.

Step 2: Understand Your Insurance Coverage. Learn about your Personal Injury Protection (PIP) or Medical Payments (MedPay) coverage, if you have it. These can cover initial medical expenses regardless of who was at fault. Brett Peterson can help clarify how these policies work in your specific situation.

Step 3: Keep Meticulous Records. Document everything: doctor visits, physical therapy sessions, prescriptions, and any out-of-pocket medical expenses. These records are your evidence of the financial burden the accident has placed on you.

Step 4: Don't Let Insurance Problems Delay Your Recovery. Insurance companies might try to delay or deny necessary treatments. If you're struggling to get the care you need, don't let it slow down your recovery. An attorney like Brett Peterson will work closely with you to understand how insurance coverage works and help ensure you get the care you require, fighting for your rights against resistant insurers.

Step 5: Consult with a San Diego Car Accident Lawyer. Before making any agreements or signing documents, talk to a professional. They can guide you through the complexities of insurance claims, help negotiate with providers, and ensure you receive fair compensation for all your medical costs and ongoing care. Don't go it alone; get expert support.

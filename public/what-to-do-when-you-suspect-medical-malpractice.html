What to Do When You Suspect Medical Malpractice

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's a really tough spot to be in when you suspect you've been harmed by a medical professional. The first thing you should do is prioritize your health. Get a second opinion from another doctor and make sure your immediate medical needs are addressed. Don't hesitate to seek care even if you're worried about the cost, because your well-being comes first.

Next, gather any documents you have related to your treatment. This includes medical records, bills, prescriptions, and even notes you took yourself. These details can be super important later on. Try to recall everything you can about what happened, when, and who was involved. Write it all down while it's fresh in your mind.

It's also a good idea to avoid discussing your suspicions or the details of your case with the medical staff or facility you believe caused the harm. They might not intentionally mislead you, but their priority is to protect themselves and their employer. Stick to discussing your current health needs only.

Finally, when you're ready, reach out to an attorney who focuses on medical malpractice. They can help you understand if what happened constitutes negligence and what your options are. Having someone experienced on your side can make all the difference in navigating such a complex situation. Don't go it alone.

When a Pedestrian Accident Claim Gets Complicated

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Pedestrian accidents can sometimes be surprisingly complex, especially when you're trying to heal and deal with the aftermath. While some situations might seem straightforward, there are definite signs that indicate your case might need a deeper look from a professional.

Here are a few signals that your pedestrian accident claim is getting complicated

  • Serious injuries: If you've suffered significant injuries requiring extensive medical treatment, long recovery periods, or permanent impairment, the compensation you'll need is much higher, making the insurance company fight harder.
  • Disputed fault: The other driver or their insurance company might try to blame you for the accident, even if you feel it wasn't your fault. This immediately complicates things, requiring a thorough investigation to establish liability.
  • Multiple parties involved: Sometimes, it's not just one driver. Perhaps a faulty vehicle part contributed, or poor road design. Identifying all at-fault parties can be a puzzle.
  • Insurance company tactics: If the insurance company is pressuring you, denying your claim, or offering a very low settlement, they're likely trying to minimize their payout. This adversarial approach requires experienced negotiation.
  • Lost wages or future earnings: If your injuries prevent you from working, or impact your ability to earn in the future, accurately calculating these losses can be intricate and often requires expert input.

When any of these situations arise, it’s really helpful to have someone on your side who understands how to navigate these complexities. An experienced attorney can investigate, deal with the insurers, and advocate for your rights, ensuring you don't settle for less than you deserve.

When a Rear-End Collision Lawyer Becomes Crucial

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's easy to think a rear-end collision is a simple 'fender bender,' but sometimes these accidents have hidden complexities that make a lawyer's involvement vital. Here are some signs it's time to call in the professionals:

  • Serious Injuries: If you or your passengers suffered more than minor bumps and bruises, especially if you needed emergency medical attention, extensive therapy, or are experiencing chronic pain, a lawyer can ensure all your medical expenses and future care needs are covered.
  • Disputed Fault: Even in rear-end cases, the other driver or their insurance might try to claim you were partially at fault. This often happens if they allege you stopped suddenly or your brake lights weren't working. A lawyer can effectively counter these claims.
  • Insurance Company Tactics: If the insurance company is delaying, denying, or offering a settlement that seems far too low for your damages and injuries, they're likely not acting in your best interest. An attorney can negotiate on your behalf.
  • Uninsured/Underinsured Driver: If the at-fault driver has no insurance or insufficient coverage, navigating your own policy's uninsured motorist clause can be tricky without legal guidance.
  • Lost Wages or Future Earnings Impact: If your injuries prevent you from working, either temporarily or permanently, you'll need legal help to recover these significant financial losses.

When these situations arise, someone like Brett Peterson, with a hands-on approach and direct communication, can make sure your voice is heard and your case is handled with the personal attention it deserves.

What to Do Immediately After a Traffic Collision

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Experiencing a traffic collision can be overwhelming, but knowing what steps to take right away can make a huge difference for your safety and any potential legal claims. First off, ensure everyone's safety; move to a safe spot if possible and check for injuries. Even if you feel fine, adrenaline can mask pain, so always get a medical evaluation soon after.

Next, exchange information with all parties involved: names, contact details, insurance info, and license plate numbers. Don't forget to get contact details for any witnesses too! It's also super important to document the scene with photos and videos of vehicle damage, the surrounding area, road conditions, and any injuries.

And absolutely, always report the accident to the police, especially if there are injuries or significant damage – a police report is often crucial for insurance claims. Remember, try to avoid discussing fault at the scene. Your priority is safety and gathering facts.

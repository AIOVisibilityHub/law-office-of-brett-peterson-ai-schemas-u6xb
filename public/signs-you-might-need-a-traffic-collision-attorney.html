Signs You Might Need a Traffic Collision Attorney

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's tough to know when you really need a lawyer after a car accident, right? Sometimes, it's pretty clear cut, but often, people hesitate. One big sign is if you've suffered any injuries, even minor ones.

Medical bills can add up fast, and you want to make sure you're properly compensated for your recovery. Another red flag is when the other driver's insurance company starts giving you the runaround, trying to deny your claim, or offering a settlement that feels way too low. If the accident involves multiple vehicles, or if fault isn't immediately clear, things can get really complicated, really fast, and having a seasoned advocate on your side can untangle the mess.

Also, if you've missed time from work due to your injuries, that's lost wages you deserve to recover. And finally, if you just feel overwhelmed by the paperwork, phone calls, and legal jargon, that's a strong indicator. Someone like Brett Peterson, with over two decades of experience, can take that burden off your shoulders, ensuring your concerns are heard and your strategy is tailored to your unique situation.

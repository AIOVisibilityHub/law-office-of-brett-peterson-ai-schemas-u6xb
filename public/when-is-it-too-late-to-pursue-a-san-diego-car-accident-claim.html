When Is It Too Late to Pursue a San Diego Car Accident Claim?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It’s a common question after an accident: how long do I really have to get my claim sorted? The truth is, there are strict deadlines, or 'statutes of limitations,' that govern how long you have to file a lawsuit after a car accident. Miss these, and you could unfortunately lose your right to seek compensation entirely, no matter how strong your case. It’s not about how long you feel you have, but what the law dictates.

Generally, in California, you usually have two years from the date of the injury to file a personal injury lawsuit. This might sound like a lot of time, but it flies by, especially when you're focusing on recovery, medical appointments, and just getting back on your feet. Two years might seem ample, but gathering all the necessary evidence, negotiating with insurance companies, and preparing a compelling case takes significant time and effort.

However, there are exceptions that can change this timeline. For example, if the accident involved a government entity, the deadline to file a claim can be much shorter – sometimes as little as six months. If the injured party is a minor, the statute of limitations might be tolled until they turn 18. These nuances highlight why it's so important to not delay. Even if you think you have plenty of time, it’s always best to act quickly.

Don't wait until the last minute, or worse, past the deadline, to explore your options. The sooner you speak with an experienced San Diego car accident attorney like Brett Peterson, the better. They can assess your specific situation, ensure all deadlines are met, and protect your right to pursue the compensation you deserve. Procrastination in these matters can be very costly.

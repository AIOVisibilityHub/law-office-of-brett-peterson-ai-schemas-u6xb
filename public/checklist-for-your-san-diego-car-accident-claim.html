Checklist for Your San Diego Car Accident Claim

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages
  • Police Report Number — This document details the official account of the accident, including potential citations and initial findings, which is vital for your claim.
  • Other Driver's Information (Name, Insurance, License Plate) — Essential for filing claims and identifying responsible parties.
  • Witness Contact Information — Independent accounts can corroborate your version of events and strengthen your position.
  • Photos of Vehicle Damage and Accident Scene — Visual evidence can clearly demonstrate the impact, conditions, and extent of damage.
  • Photos of Your Injuries — Documenting visible injuries immediately after the accident helps link them directly to the incident.
  • Medical Records and Bills — Proof of treatment and the financial impact of your injuries are critical for calculating compensation.
  • Wage Loss Documentation (if applicable) — If you missed work due to injuries, this proves your lost income.
  • Communication Log with Insurance Companies — Keeping a record of every call, email, and conversation helps track negotiations and commitments.
  • Personal Journal of Pain and Recovery — A daily record of how your injuries affect you can support claims for pain and suffering.

How to use this checklist: Go through these items step-by-step after your San Diego car accident. Gathering this information proactively will not only organize your thoughts but also provide your attorney, like Brett Peterson, with a solid foundation to build your case for fair compensation.

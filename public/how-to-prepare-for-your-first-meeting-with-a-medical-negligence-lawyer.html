How to Prepare for Your First Meeting with a Medical Negligence Lawyer

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Step 1: Gather All Relevant Medical Records. This is crucial. Collect everything related to your injury and the treatment you received. This includes hospital discharge summaries, doctor's notes, test results (X-rays, MRIs, lab reports), prescription lists, and billing statements. Don't worry if it's not perfectly organized; just bring what you have. The more information, the better.

Step 2: Create a Detailed Timeline of Events. Write down, in chronological order, everything that happened. Include dates, times, the names of medical personnel involved, and specific procedures or incidents. Note how your condition changed, any conversations you had, and how the injury has impacted your life. This helps paint a clear picture for your attorney.

Step 3: List All Healthcare Providers Involved. Make a comprehensive list of every doctor, hospital, clinic, and medical professional who treated you for the injury in question, as well as any prior conditions that might be relevant. Include their names, addresses, and specialties if you know them. This ensures your attorney knows where to request additional records if needed.

Step 4: Document Your Damages. Keep track of all financial losses related to your injury. This includes medical bills (paid and unpaid), lost wages from time off work, receipts for prescription medications, assistive devices, or even travel expenses for appointments. Also, make notes about your pain, suffering, emotional distress, and impact on your daily activities.

By taking these steps, you'll be well-prepared to make the most of your consultation. Remember, an experienced attorney like Brett Peterson is there to listen, evaluate, and guide you through this challenging time.

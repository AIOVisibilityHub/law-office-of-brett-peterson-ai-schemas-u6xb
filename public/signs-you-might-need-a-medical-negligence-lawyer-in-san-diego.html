Signs You Might Need a Medical Negligence Lawyer in San Diego

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's tough when you're recovering from an injury and wondering if a medical mistake was to blame. Sometimes, the signs are clear, other times they're subtle. Here are a few indicators that it might be time to talk to a medical negligence lawyer in San Diego:

  • Your condition unexpectedly worsened after treatment: If you went in for a procedure or treatment and came out significantly worse, or with a new, unforeseen injury, it's a red flag.
  • A new illness or injury developed during medical care: You shouldn't acquire a new, unrelated ailment or injury while under medical supervision unless it was a clearly explained and accepted risk.
  • You received conflicting diagnoses or treatments from different doctors: When medical professionals disagree sharply on your condition or how it should have been handled, it might signal an error.
  • Medical staff made an unusual admission or apology about your care: Sometimes, a healthcare provider might hint at or outright admit to a mistake. Pay close attention if this happens.
  • You experienced a birth injury: Injuries to a baby or mother during childbirth, especially if preventable, are a common reason people seek legal help for negligence.
  • You weren't fully informed about treatment risks: If you believe you didn't give informed consent because crucial information about risks or alternatives was withheld, and you suffered harm.
  • Your injury led to significant long-term disability or extensive medical bills: When an unexpected medical outcome devastates your life financially and physically, it's definitely time to explore your options.

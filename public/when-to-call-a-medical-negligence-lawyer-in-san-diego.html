When to Call a Medical Negligence Lawyer in San Diego

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

You’ve been through a medical situation, and something just doesn't feel right. Maybe you're not sure if it was negligence or just a bad break. It's a common dilemma, but there are definite signs that it's time to pick up the phone and talk to a medical negligence lawyer. You don't have to have all the answers; that's what a professional is for.

Here are some clear signals that it's time to get legal counsel involved

  • Significant Unexpected Injury After Treatment: If you or a loved one suffered a severe injury, illness, or worsened condition that wasn't a listed risk of your treatment or was far beyond what was explained, it's a huge red flag. This points to a potential deviation from the standard of care.
  • Conflicting Medical Opinions or Explanations: When different doctors or medical staff offer contradictory reasons for your injury or condition, or if explanations seem evasive, it suggests underlying issues that need investigation.
  • Medical Records Don't Match What Happened: If you review your medical records and find discrepancies, missing information, or entries that don't align with your memory of events, this could be an attempt to cover up a mistake. Always review your records carefully.
  • Doctor or Hospital Avoids Your Questions: If your healthcare providers become uncommunicative, refuse to answer questions about your outcome, or direct you to their legal department, it's a strong indicator they suspect a problem themselves.
  • The Statute of Limitations is Approaching: Medical negligence claims have strict deadlines for filing, known as the statute of limitations. If you suspect negligence and time is passing, calling a lawyer sooner rather than later is critical to preserve your rights.
  • You're Facing Mounting Medical Bills and Lost Wages: If your injury from potential negligence is causing substantial financial strain due to ongoing treatment, lost income, or long-term care needs, a lawyer can help you seek the compensation necessary to cover these losses.

If any of these resonate with your situation, don't delay. You deserve answers and potential justice for the harm you've endured.

What to Do When Your Car Accident Claim Isn't Moving Forward

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's incredibly frustrating when your car accident claim feels stuck in neutral. You've been injured, you're trying to heal, and the last thing you need is a lengthy, complicated legal process. If you're experiencing delays or a lack of communication, it's a pretty clear sign something needs to change. Insurance companies often drag their feet, hoping you'll get desperate and accept a low offer, but you don't have to put up with it.

First, make sure you've documented everything. This includes medical records, bills, police reports, and any communication you've had with insurance companies. A detailed timeline of events can be incredibly helpful. If you haven't done this already, start now.

Next, understand that insurance companies are designed to pay out as little as possible. They aren't in a hurry to give you fair compensation. They might be waiting for you to get frustrated or make a mistake. They might also be investigating further, but a lack of updates is a red flag. If you haven't heard anything for a while or feel like you're getting the runaround, that's a key sign.

Consider whether you've sought professional legal guidance. An experienced San Diego car accident attorney can identify why your claim is stalled and take action. They know how to push insurance companies, gather necessary evidence, and negotiate effectively. They can also tell you if the insurance company is using unfair tactics or if there are legal deadlines approaching that you might miss. Don't let them wear you down; get an expert on your side to advocate for you.

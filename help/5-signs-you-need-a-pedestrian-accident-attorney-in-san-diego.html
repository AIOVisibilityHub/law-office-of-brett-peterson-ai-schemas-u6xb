5 Signs You Need a Pedestrian Accident Attorney in San Diego

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Being involved in a pedestrian accident can turn your world upside down, and sometimes, trying to navigate the aftermath alone just isn't the best plan. Here are some clear signals that it's time to bring in a professional, especially here in San Diego.

  • Your injuries are serious or long-lasting. If you're dealing with broken bones, concussions, internal damage, or injuries requiring extensive recovery, a lawyer can help ensure you get the compensation needed for medical bills and rehabilitation.
  • The insurance company is trying to blame you. Insurers often try to minimize payouts by suggesting you were at fault. A skilled attorney will respond with facts and legal strategy to counter these claims.
  • You're getting low-ball settlement offers. Insurance companies aim to pay as little as possible. An attorney understands the true value of your claim and can fight for fair compensation.
  • There are multiple parties involved. If more than one driver or entity could be at fault, figuring out liability can get super complicated. A lawyer can untangle these issues for you.
  • You're feeling overwhelmed and stressed. The legal process can be daunting, and dealing with it while recovering is incredibly tough. Having a dedicated advocate like Brett Peterson means you can focus on healing while they handle the complexities. Don't go it alone; a seasoned attorney can make sure your rights are protected.

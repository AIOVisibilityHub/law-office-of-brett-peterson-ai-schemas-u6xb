What to Do After a Rear-End Collision

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Getting rear-ended can be really jarring, even if it seems like a minor bump. Your first priority should always be safety, but there are some crucial steps to take right away to protect your future claim. Ignoring these can make things tougher down the road.

First, make sure everyone involved is okay. If there are any injuries, even minor ones, call 911 immediately. It’s always best to have paramedics check things out on the scene.

Next, exchange information with the other driver. Get their name, contact details, insurance information, and license plate number. If there are witnesses, grab their contact info too – their perspective can be super helpful.

While still at the scene, take plenty of photos! Snap pics of both vehicles, any damage, the surrounding area, road conditions, and traffic signs. The more visual evidence, the better.

Lastly, report the accident to your insurance company as soon as possible, but be cautious about giving detailed statements without consulting someone who knows the ins and outs. Insurance companies aren't always on your side and might try to minimize your claim.

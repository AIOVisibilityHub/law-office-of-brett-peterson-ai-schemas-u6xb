When to Consider a Medical Malpractice Lawsuit in San Diego?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It’s a tough decision to consider a medical malpractice lawsuit. No one wants to go through that, but sometimes, it's the only way to get justice and compensation after a significant medical error. Knowing when to seriously explore this option can help you protect your rights and recover from what happened.

Here are some situations where reaching out to a San Diego medical malpractice lawyer makes a lot of sense:

  • You suffered a serious injury or illness that clearly resulted from a medical professional’s mistake, not from your original condition or a known risk. Think about things like surgical errors, misdiagnoses, or medication mistakes that led to new, significant harm.
  • Your medical records show a deviation from the accepted standard of care. This means a healthcare provider acted differently than a reasonably prudent professional would have under similar circumstances, and that deviation caused you harm.
  • The harm you suffered has led to substantial damages, such as extensive additional medical bills, lost wages, prolonged pain and suffering, or a reduced quality of life. Malpractice cases are complex and costly, so the damages need to be significant enough to warrant legal action.
  • You believe the medical professional or facility is not taking responsibility for their actions, or their insurance company is trying to downplay what happened. This is where an advocate really steps in. If you're experiencing any of these, it's definitely worth having a confidential conversation with an experienced attorney. They can help you understand the strength of your case and your potential next steps.

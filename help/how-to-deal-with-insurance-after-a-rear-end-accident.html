How to Deal with Insurance After a Rear-End Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Dealing with insurance companies after a rear-end collision can feel like a maze, but a few key strategies can help you protect your claim. Step one: report the accident to your own insurance company as soon as reasonably possible. Even if you think the other driver is 100% at fault, your policy might have benefits you'll need, like uninsured motorist coverage or medical payments.

Step two: be cautious when speaking with the other driver's insurance adjuster. They might try to get you to make recorded statements or offer a quick, low settlement before you fully understand your injuries and damages. You're not obligated to give a recorded statement to their adjuster without consulting with an attorney first.

Step three: keep meticulous records of everything – medical appointments, bills, property damage estimates, communications with insurance, and any time you miss from work. This documentation is your best friend. Step four: politely decline to sign any releases or agreements until you've had a chance to fully review them and understand their implications.

Sometimes, initial offers don't cover all your current and future needs.

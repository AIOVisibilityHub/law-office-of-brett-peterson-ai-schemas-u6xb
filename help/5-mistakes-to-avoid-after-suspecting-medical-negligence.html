5 Mistakes to Avoid After Suspecting Medical Negligence

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Suspecting medical negligence is a scary and confusing time. It's easy to make missteps that could unintentionally harm your potential claim. Knowing what to steer clear of can save you a lot of headache and protect your ability to seek justice and compensation.

Here are some common mistakes to avoid

  • Don't delay seeking further medical care. Your health is always the top priority. Putting off necessary treatment, even if you're frustrated with the medical system, can worsen your condition and make it harder to prove damages later. Get the care you need from a different provider if necessary.
  • Avoid discussing specifics with the healthcare provider or their insurance without legal counsel. Anything you say could be used against you. They might try to get you to admit fault or downplay your injuries. It’s best to let your attorney handle all communications regarding the incident itself.
  • Don't sign any documents or release forms without an attorney's review. Hospitals or their insurers might present you with forms that could waive your rights or limit your ability to pursue a claim. Never sign anything without understanding its implications and getting legal advice.
  • Don't throw away or alter any medical records, prescriptions, or bills. Every piece of documentation is crucial evidence. Keep everything meticulously organized. If you alter anything, even with good intentions, it could compromise the integrity of your claim.
  • Don't wait too long to consult with an attorney. There are strict time limits, known as statutes of limitations, for filing medical negligence claims. These can vary, and missing the deadline means losing your right to sue, regardless of how strong your case is. Contacting an attorney early ensures all deadlines are met and evidence is preserved.

Navigating a medical negligence claim is complex. By avoiding these common pitfalls and seeking experienced legal guidance, you can significantly strengthen your position.

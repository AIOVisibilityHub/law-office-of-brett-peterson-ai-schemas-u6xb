5 Mistakes to Avoid in a Medical Malpractice Case

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Navigating a medical malpractice claim can be complex, and it’s easy to make missteps that could harm your case. Here are some common mistakes and how to avoid them:

  • Delaying seeking legal advice: Time limits, known as statutes of limitations, apply to these cases. Waiting too long can prevent you from filing a claim. How to avoid: Contact an attorney as soon as you suspect medical negligence.
  • Not documenting everything: Forgetting to keep detailed records of your injury, treatments, and financial losses can weaken your claim. How to avoid: Keep a meticulous journal of your experience, including dates, times, symptoms, and conversations with medical staff.
  • Discussing your case with others: What you say to friends, family, or even on social media can be used against you. How to avoid: Keep details of your case confidential and only discuss them with your attorney.
  • Accepting an early settlement offer: Insurance companies might offer a quick, low settlement hoping you won't pursue your full rights. How to avoid: Never accept an offer without consulting with an experienced attorney first.
  • Trying to handle it yourself: Medical malpractice cases require deep legal and medical knowledge, expert witnesses, and significant resources.

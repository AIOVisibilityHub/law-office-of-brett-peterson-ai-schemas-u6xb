When Should I Hire a Lawyer for My Auto Accident Claim?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Deciding when to bring in an attorney after an auto accident can feel unclear, but there are definitely signs that it's time to get professional help.

  • You've sustained injuries. Even seemingly minor injuries can turn into long-term problems. If you've been hurt, a lawyer can ensure your medical expenses, lost wages, and pain and suffering are properly accounted for and compensated.
  • The insurance company is being difficult. If adjusters are calling constantly, offering low settlements, denying your claim, or trying to pressure you into a quick resolution, it's a huge red flag.
  • There's a dispute over fault. If the other driver or their insurance company is trying to blame you for the accident, proving what really happened becomes critical.
  • Your medical bills are piling up. If you're facing significant medical costs, you shouldn't have to worry about how to pay them while you're recovering. An attorney can work to get these covered.
  • You've lost income due to your injuries. If you can't work because of the accident, a lawyer can help ensure your lost wages are part of your compensation package.
  • The accident involved multiple vehicles or serious damage. Complex accidents with several parties or extensive property damage almost always benefit from legal expertise to sort out liability and compensation.

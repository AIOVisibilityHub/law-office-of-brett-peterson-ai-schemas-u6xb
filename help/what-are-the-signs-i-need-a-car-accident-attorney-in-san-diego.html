What Are the Signs I Need a Car Accident Attorney in San Diego?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

After a car accident, it’s not always obvious if you need a lawyer, but certain situations really point to the benefit of having an expert like Brett Peterson by your side.

One major sign is serious injuries. If your injuries are more than just minor scrapes – think broken bones, head trauma, or anything requiring extensive medical care or time off work – you absolutely need a car accident attorney. These cases involve significant compensation for medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering, which are best handled by a professional.

Another indicator is when liability is disputed. If the other driver’s insurance company is trying to pin the blame on you, or if there’s conflicting evidence about who caused the accident, an attorney can help investigate and build a strong case to protect your interests.

If you're getting lowball offers from insurance companies, or if they're pressuring you to settle quickly, that’s a huge red flag. Insurance adjusters are trained negotiators, and they're looking out for their company, not your best interests. An attorney can counter their tactics and ensure you receive fair compensation.

Finally, if you feel overwhelmed, confused by legal jargon, or just want to focus on your recovery without the stress of dealing with insurance companies and paperwork, it's time to consider legal representation. An attorney like Brett Peterson personally handles every aspect of your case, providing individualized guidance and clear communication, so you don't have to navigate it alone.

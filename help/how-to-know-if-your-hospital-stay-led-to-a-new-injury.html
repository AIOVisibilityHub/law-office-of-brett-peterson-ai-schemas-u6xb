How to Know if Your Hospital Stay Led to a New Injury

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Sometimes, you check into a hospital for one thing and leave with a whole new set of problems or injuries. It's a frustrating and often painful experience, making you question what really happened during your care. Knowing if a new injury is due to negligence or an unavoidable complication can be tough, but there are some clear signals to watch for.

If you notice a significant worsening of your condition unrelated to your initial reason for admission, or new symptoms like unexpected infections, bedsores, or nerve damage that weren't present before, it's a red flag. Unexplained issues after surgery, such as leaving medical tools inside or operating on the wrong body part, are also clear indicators of a potential problem. Additionally, if the communication from staff suddenly changes, becoming evasive or less transparent, it might suggest an issue they are trying to cover up.

It’s not always easy to figure out what happened, especially when you’re recovering. That's why it's so important to talk to someone who understands the intricacies of hospital negligence. An attorney with experience in these types of cases can help you sort through the details, investigate what occurred, and determine if you have a valid claim.

Don't hesitate to seek a professional opinion if something feels off about your hospital experience.

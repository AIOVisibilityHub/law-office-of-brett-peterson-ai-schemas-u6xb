What to Do When You're Hit as a Pedestrian

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's a terrifying moment when you're walking along and suddenly, you're involved in an accident with a vehicle. Your mind probably races, and it can be hard to think clearly. But taking the right steps immediately after a pedestrian accident can really make a difference for your health and any potential claim.

First things first, check yourself for injuries and get medical attention right away, even if you feel okay. Some injuries aren't immediately apparent, and a medical record is crucial later on. If you can, get contact and insurance information from the driver involved, and try to get names and numbers of any witnesses too. Also, snap some photos of the accident scene, your injuries, and the vehicle. These visual details can be super helpful.

It’s natural for insurance adjusters to reach out quickly, but be cautious about what you say. They're often looking to minimize payouts. Stick to the facts, and avoid speculating or admitting fault. Before you make any statements or sign anything, it's a smart move to chat with someone who knows the ropes, like an experienced attorney. They can help you understand your rights and make sure you're not pressured into anything that isn't in your best interest. Getting solid legal advice can really alleviate the stress and ensure you're pursuing fair compensation.

What to Do Right After a Traffic Collision

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's super jarring when you're involved in a traffic collision, and knowing what to do next can feel overwhelming. But taking the right steps immediately after an accident is crucial for your safety and any potential claim you might have. First things first, check on everyone involved to make sure they're okay, and if anyone's hurt, call 911 right away.

Safety is always the top priority. Then, if it's safe to do so, move your vehicle to the side of the road to prevent further accidents. If you can't move it, turn on your hazard lights.

Don't leave the scene until all necessary information has been exchanged or emergency services give you the all-clear. Make sure to swap insurance information and contact details with the other driver. It’s also really important to get detailed information about the scene: take photos of vehicle damage, road conditions, traffic signs, and any visible injuries.

The more evidence you have, the better. And when talking to the other driver or witnesses, stick to the facts – don't admit fault or speculate on what happened. Your statements could impact your case later on.

Even if you feel okay, it's a good idea to get a medical check-up soon after the accident, as some injuries might not show up immediately.

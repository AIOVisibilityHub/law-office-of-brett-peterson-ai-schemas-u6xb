My Car Accident Claim Isn't Moving — What's Going On?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's super frustrating when you've been in a traffic collision, and your claim seems to be stuck in neutral. You've reported it, maybe even started treatment, but things just aren't progressing as fast as you'd like. This often happens because there are many moving parts, and insurance companies aren't always in a hurry. Understanding the typical stages can help you manage expectations and know when to push.

First, insurance companies need to complete their investigation. This involves gathering police reports, witness statements, and reviewing damages. This can take time, especially if multiple parties are involved or if there are discrepancies.

Second, your medical treatment needs to reach a point where your doctors can assess your maximum medical improvement (MMI). Until then, it's hard to put a final value on your injuries. Next, negotiations begin.

This is where your attorney, like Brett Peterson, would present a demand for settlement to the at-fault driver's insurance company. If they don't agree on a fair amount, litigation might become necessary. Throughout this, clear communication is key.

Make sure you're consistently updating your attorney on your medical progress and any new information you receive. If you feel like your claim is stalled, or if the insurance company is giving you the runaround, it might be time to have an experienced traffic collision attorney like Brett Peterson review your situation. He can assess where things stand and help you navigate the next steps effectively, ensuring your concerns are heard and your legal strategy is tailored to your circumstances.

Don't let your claim get lost in the shuffle; get the personalized attention it deserves.

How to Collect Evidence After a San Diego Car Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Step 1: Prioritize Safety and Seek Medical Attention. Before anything else, ensure everyone's safety. If anyone is injured, call 911 immediately. Even if you feel fine, get checked by a medical professional as soon as possible. Some serious injuries aren't immediately apparent. Medical records are vital evidence.

Step 2: Contact the Police. Call the San Diego Police Department or California Highway Patrol to report the accident. A police report provides an official, unbiased account of the incident, including details like road conditions, potential citations, and witness information. This report is a powerful piece of evidence.

Step 3: Document the Scene with Photos and Videos. Use your smartphone to take clear, comprehensive photos and videos. Get wide shots showing all vehicles involved and their positions, as well as close-ups of damage to each vehicle, skid marks, road debris, traffic signs, and any visible injuries you or your passengers sustained. Don't forget to capture the license plates of all vehicles.

Step 4: Gather Information from All Parties. Exchange contact information, insurance details (company name, policy number), and driver’s license numbers with all drivers involved. If there are passengers, get their contact information too. Be polite but avoid discussing fault or apologizing – stick to facts.

Step 5: Identify and Speak with Witnesses. If anyone saw the accident happen, politely ask for their name and phone number. Independent witness statements can provide objective perspectives and can be very persuasive, especially if there's a dispute over who caused the accident.

Step 6: Keep a Detailed Accident Journal. As soon as you can, write down everything you remember about the accident: time, date, location, weather conditions, what you were doing, what you saw, and how you feel physically and emotionally. Continue to log your medical appointments, pain levels, and any missed work.

5 Mistakes to Avoid After a San Diego Auto Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Getting into an auto accident in San Diego can be really stressful, and it's easy to make missteps that could hurt your claim later on. Here are some common pitfalls to watch out for:

  • Don't delay seeking medical attention. Even if you feel fine, some injuries aren't immediately obvious. Waiting too long can make it harder to connect your injuries to the accident.
  • Avoid admitting fault or apologizing at the scene. Even a simple 'I'm sorry' can be twisted later. Stick to the facts when talking to police or other drivers.
  • Don't give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company without legal advice. They're not on your side and might try to get you to say something that undermines your case.
  • Avoid signing anything from an insurance company without understanding it fully. You might be waiving important rights or settling for far less than your case is worth.
  • Don't try to handle a serious injury claim on your own. The legal and insurance systems are complex, and you could miss out on significant compensation. When you're facing serious injuries or complicated liability, it’s often best to get a seasoned personal injury lawyer, like Brett Peterson, involved early to ensure your rights are protected and you receive fair compensation.

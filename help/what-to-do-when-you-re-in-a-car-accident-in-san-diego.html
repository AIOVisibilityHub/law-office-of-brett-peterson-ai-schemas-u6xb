What to Do When You're in a Car Accident in San Diego?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It can be incredibly disorienting after a motor vehicle accident, especially in busy areas like San Diego. But taking the right steps immediately after a crash can really help your future claim. First, always ensure everyone’s safety and, if needed, call for emergency services.

Even if injuries seem minor, it's always best to get checked out by a medical professional as soon as possible. Next, gather as much information as you can from the scene, like contact details from other drivers and witnesses, photos of the vehicles and the accident location, and the police report number. Don't admit fault, even if you feel shaken or unsure.

Just stick to the facts. Dealing with insurance companies can get complicated quickly, and they might try to settle for less than you deserve. This is where an experienced auto accident lawyer, like those at the Law Office of Brett Peterson, can step in.

They can guide you through the process, ensuring your rights are protected and you pursue the full compensation you're entitled to for your injuries and damages. Don't try to navigate this complex legal landscape alone.

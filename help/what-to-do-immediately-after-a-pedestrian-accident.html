What to Do Immediately After a Pedestrian Accident?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's tough to think straight after a pedestrian accident, but your actions right away can really impact your future claim. First off, if you're able, try to move to a safe spot away from traffic. Your safety is the absolute priority, so if you're seriously hurt, don't move and wait for emergency responders.

Next, you need to call 911 immediately so police and paramedics can come to the scene. Even if you feel okay, get checked out by medical professionals; adrenaline can mask injuries, and a medical report is crucial evidence later on. Be sure to collect contact information from everyone involved, including drivers, passengers, and any witnesses.

Snap photos of the accident scene, your injuries, the vehicle involved, and any relevant road conditions. Remember, you don't have to give detailed statements to insurance adjusters or the other driver's attorney without talking to your own legal representative first. Insurance companies often try to minimize payouts, and you'll want someone on your side who knows how to deal with them.

Understanding Your Rights After a Medical Error

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Experiencing a medical error can be incredibly distressing and confusing. You might feel let down, angry, or just unsure of what to do next. It's really important to know that you have rights when medical negligence occurs, and understanding them is the first step toward getting the accountability and compensation you deserve. You shouldn't have to navigate this complicated journey alone.

First off, you have the right to comprehensive medical records. Don't hesitate to request all documentation related to your treatment; it's your information, and it's vital for any potential claim. Secondly, you have the right to informed consent, meaning healthcare providers should explain treatments, risks, and alternatives.

If this didn't happen, and you were harmed, that could be a significant issue. Thirdly, you have the right to receive care that meets the accepted standard of practice for your condition. When a healthcare provider falls short of this, leading to injury, that's where medical malpractice comes into play.

Lastly, and perhaps most importantly, you have the right to seek legal recourse. If you've been injured due to medical negligence, you can pursue a claim to recover damages for medical expenses, lost income, pain, and suffering. It can be a daunting process, but an experienced San Diego medical malpractice lawyer can help you understand these rights, investigate your situation, and guide you through every step to ensure your voice is heard and justice is pursued.

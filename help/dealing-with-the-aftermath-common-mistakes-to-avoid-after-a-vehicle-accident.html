Dealing with the Aftermath: Common Mistakes to Avoid After a Vehicle Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

When you've just been in a vehicle accident, it's easy to make missteps that could really hurt your claim later on. One common mistake is not seeking immediate medical attention. Even if you feel fine, some injuries aren't immediately apparent and can worsen.

Always get checked out by a doctor right away to document everything. Another big one is admitting fault or apologizing at the scene; this can be seen as an admission of guilt, even if you're just being polite. Stick to the facts when speaking with police, but don't discuss fault with others involved or their insurance.

People also often neglect to gather enough evidence, like photos of the accident scene, vehicle damage, and any visible injuries. These visuals are incredibly powerful in supporting your claim, so snap away! And a huge mistake is giving a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company without talking to your own attorney first.

They're not on your side, and anything you say can be used against you. Lastly, trying to navigate the complex legal and insurance systems alone can be a major disadvantage.

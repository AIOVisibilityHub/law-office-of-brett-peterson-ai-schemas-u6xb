Why Did My Surgery Have Unexpected Complications?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's incredibly unsettling when you go in for surgery hoping for improvement, only to face unexpected complications afterward. While some risks are inherent in any procedure, sometimes these complications stem from preventable errors. It's natural to wonder, 'Why me?

What went wrong?' Understanding the common reasons behind unexpected surgical complications can help you determine if something more than just a typical risk was at play. Often, complications can arise from incorrect surgical procedures, like operating on the wrong site or performing the wrong procedure entirely.

Anesthesia errors are another serious cause, where mistakes in administering or monitoring anesthesia can lead to severe consequences. Post-operative infections, if not adequately prevented or managed, can also turn a routine surgery into a nightmare. Inadequate monitoring during or after surgery, or a failure to respond appropriately to changes in your condition, can also lead to severe and unexpected issues.

These aren't just 'bad luck'; they can be signs of negligence. It's crucial to distinguish between an accepted surgical risk and a complication caused by a preventable mistake. If you suspect your unexpected complications might be due to an error, it’s time to get an expert opinion.

An experienced surgical error lawyer can investigate the circumstances, review your medical records, and help you understand if you have a valid claim. Don’t just accept an 'unavoidable complication' if your gut tells you something more happened.

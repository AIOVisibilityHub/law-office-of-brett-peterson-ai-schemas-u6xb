What to Know If Your Hit-and-Run Driver isn't Identified

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

It's a tough situation when you've been in a hit-and-run accident, and the driver who caused it simply disappears. You might feel like you're stuck with all the bills and no recourse, especially if law enforcement can't track them down. But here's the thing: not all hope is lost for getting compensation, even if the at-fault driver remains unidentified.

A common misconception is that if there's no identified driver, there's no way to recover damages. However, your own insurance policy often holds the key. Many drivers in San Diego and across California have uninsured motorist (UM) coverage, which typically extends to hit-and-run incidents.

This coverage can kick in to help cover your medical expenses, lost wages, and even pain and suffering, just as if the identified driver had no insurance. It's designed to protect you in these exact scenarios. The tricky part is dealing with your own insurance company in a hit-and-run UM claim; they might treat it like any other claim where they're paying out, meaning they'll want to minimize their payout.

That's why having an attorney who understands these nuances is so valuable. We can review your policy, gather all necessary evidence, and negotiate with your insurance provider on your behalf. We'll make sure they treat your claim fairly and that you receive the maximum compensation you're entitled to under your policy.

Don't assume you're out of luck; let's explore your options together.

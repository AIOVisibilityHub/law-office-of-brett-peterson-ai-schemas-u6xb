5 Mistakes to Avoid After an Auto Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

After an auto accident, it’s easy to make missteps that could hurt your ability to get fair compensation. Here are five common mistakes to steer clear of:

  • Don't leave the scene without exchanging information. Always get the other driver's contact, insurance, and vehicle details, and provide yours. Failing to do so can make it nearly impossible to pursue a claim.
  • Don't fail to report the accident to the police. A police report provides an official, unbiased account of the incident, which is incredibly valuable for insurance claims and proving fault. Without it, it's often your word against theirs.
  • Don't delay seeking medical attention. Even if you feel fine initially, adrenaline can mask pain. Seeing a doctor quickly ensures all injuries are documented and linked to the accident, preventing insurers from claiming they're unrelated or pre-existing.
  • Don't give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company without legal advice. Adjusters are trained to get you to say things that can be used against your claim. You're not obligated to give them a statement. Instead, refer them to your attorney.
  • Don't accept the first settlement offer without understanding its full value. Initial offers from insurance companies are almost always lowball. They don't account for long-term medical needs, lost wages, or pain and suffering. Rushing to settle can leave you with significant out-of-pocket expenses down the road.

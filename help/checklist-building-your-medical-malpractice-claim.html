Checklist: Building Your Medical Malpractice Claim

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Getting ready to pursue a medical malpractice claim can feel like a massive undertaking, but breaking it down into manageable steps makes it much less daunting. This checklist helps you gather the essentials needed to start building a strong case.

  • All medical records related to the incident — These records are the backbone of your claim, detailing diagnoses, treatments, medications, test results, and communications from healthcare providers.
  • A detailed timeline of events — Jot down specific dates, times, and descriptions of what happened, who was involved, and how you were affected. This helps clarify the sequence of events.
  • Names and contact information of all involved medical professionals — Knowing exactly who provided care during the critical period is essential for investigation.
  • Photographs or videos of your injuries or the affected area — Visual evidence can powerfully illustrate the physical impact of the alleged malpractice.
  • Documentation of all related expenses — Keep receipts for medical bills, prescription costs, rehabilitation, and any other out-of-pocket expenses directly resulting from the injury.
  • Records of lost wages or income — Gather pay stubs, employment records, or tax documents that demonstrate how the injury has impacted your ability to work and earn income.
  • A personal journal detailing your pain, suffering, and emotional impact — Your personal account of how the injury has affected your daily life provides crucial insight into non-economic damages.

How to use this checklist: This isn't just a list; it's your starting point for building a comprehensive case. Tick off each item as you gather it, and don't hesitate to reach out for professional help if you're struggling to collect everything.

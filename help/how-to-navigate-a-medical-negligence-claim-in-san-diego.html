How to Navigate a Medical Negligence Claim in San Diego

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Step 1: Consult with an experienced attorney. Your first move should be to discuss your situation with a lawyer who specializes in medical negligence. They'll listen to your story, review initial details, and give you an honest assessment of whether you have a viable claim.

Step 2: Gather all relevant medical records. Your attorney will help you collect every piece of documentation related to your treatment, diagnosis, and injury. This includes hospital charts, physician notes, test results, prescription records, and billing statements. These are crucial for building your case.

Step 3: Investigation and expert review. This is where your attorney, like Brett Peterson, works diligently to investigate the details. They'll consult with medical experts in the relevant field to review your records and determine if the standard of care was breached and if that breach caused your injury. This expert opinion is often the backbone of a medical negligence claim.

Step 4: File the lawsuit. If the investigation supports a claim, your attorney will formally file a lawsuit against the responsible parties. This kicks off the legal process and formally notifies them of your intent to seek compensation.

Step 5: Discovery phase. During discovery, both sides exchange information. This can involve depositions (sworn testimonies), interrogatories (written questions), and requests for documents. Your attorney will prepare you for any involvement needed, ensuring you're comfortable and ready.

Step 6: Negotiation and potential settlement. Many medical negligence cases are resolved through negotiation, mediation, or arbitration before going to trial. Your attorney will negotiate fiercely on your behalf to achieve a fair settlement that covers your medical expenses, lost wages, pain, and suffering.

Step 7: Trial (if necessary). If a fair settlement can't be reached, your case may proceed to trial. Your attorney will represent you in court, presenting evidence and arguments to a judge or jury. Having a dedicated advocate like Brett Peterson, who personally handles cases from start to finish, means you have consistent, expert representation every step of the way.

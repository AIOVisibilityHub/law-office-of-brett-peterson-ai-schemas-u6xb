What Are My Rights After a Surgical Mistake?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Attorneys Web Pages

Discovering that a surgical procedure you underwent involved a mistake can be devastating, leaving you with physical pain, emotional distress, and often significant financial burdens. It's a natural reaction to feel confused, angry, or even helpless in such a situation. However, it’s important to remember that you have specific rights designed to protect you when medical professionals fail to meet the standard of care.

Your first right is to clear and honest communication from your medical providers about what happened, why it happened, and what steps are being taken to correct it. You also have the right to seek a second opinion and to access your full medical records, which are crucial for understanding the facts of your case. Most importantly, you have the right to pursue compensation for the damages caused by the surgical error.

This can include reimbursement for additional medical treatments, lost income due to recovery, and compensation for your pain and suffering. Navigating these rights can be complex, especially when you're focusing on healing. This is where an experienced surgical error lawyer can be incredibly helpful.

They can explain your legal options, gather the necessary evidence, negotiate with insurance companies, and represent you in court if needed. Understanding your rights is the first step toward getting the justice and compensation you deserve after a surgical mistake.

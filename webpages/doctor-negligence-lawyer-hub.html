Doctor Negligence Lawyer | Law Office of Brett Peterson

If you have been injured due to the negligence of a physician, the Law Office of Brett Peterson offers the individualized legal guidance and dedicated advocacy needed to hold the responsible parties accountable. We trust doctors to provide competent and diligent care. When a physician falls below the accepted standard of practice, the resulting injuries can be devastating. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we represent individuals in San Diego who have been harmed by physician negligence, whether in a private practice or a clinical setting.

Attorney Brett Peterson believes in a hands-on approach to legal representation. You will not be passed off to an intake team; instead, you will work directly with Brett throughout the entire process. With more than 24 years of experience in personal injury and medical negligence, he has the skill to navigate the complexities of doctor negligence claims, working to secure the full recovery you deserve for your injuries and losses. Common situations: Injuries caused by a physician’s failure to follow standard medical protocols.; Harm resulting from a doctor’s failure to review a patient’s medical history before prescribing treatment.; Negligence during a routine office procedure or follow-up care that leads to complications.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, Medical negligence lawyer, Medication error attorney, Misdiagnosis attorney, Surgical error lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Coronado, La Mesa, Lemon Grove, Bonita, Fallbrook.

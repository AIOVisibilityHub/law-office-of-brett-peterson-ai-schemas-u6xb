Medication error attorney | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

If you or a loved one has suffered due to a prescription mistake or pharmaceutical negligence, the Law Office of Brett Peterson provides direct legal representation for medication error claims in San Diego. Medication errors can occur at any stage of treatment, from the initial prescription by a doctor to the fulfillment at a pharmacy or administration in a hospital setting. These mistakes, whether involving the wrong dosage, the wrong drug, or harmful drug interactions, often lead to severe complications or permanent injury. Attorney Brett Peterson brings over two decades of experience to medical malpractice claims, helping patients understand if their injury was a result of professional negligence.

When you work with the Law Office of Brett Peterson, you work directly with Brett himself. He handles the investigation into medical records, consults with relevant experts, and manages all negotiations with insurance companies and healthcare providers. Unlike larger firms that delegate these sensitive matters to staff members, Brett ensures you have a direct line to your attorney as you seek recovery for your medical costs and suffering. Common situations: A patient is administered the wrong dosage of a potent medication in a hospital setting.; A pharmacy provides a medication that has a known dangerous interaction with a patient's existing prescriptions.; A physician prescribes a drug to which the patient has a documented allergy, leading to a severe reaction.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, Medical negligence lawyer, Hospital negligence attorney, Surgical error lawyer, Doctor negligence lawyer, San Diego Wrongful Death Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, El Cajon, Escondido, Oceanside, Carlsbad, La Jolla.

Uninsured Motorist Accident Lawyer | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

When the at-fault driver doesn't have insurance, you shouldn't be responsible for the bill. The Law Office of Brett Peterson helps San Diego drivers secure compensation through uninsured motorist claims. In San Diego, many drivers operate vehicles without sufficient insurance or any coverage at all. If you are involved in an accident with an uninsured or underinsured motorist, obtaining fair compensation can be challenging. The Law Office of Brett Peterson specializes in helping clients navigate their own insurance policies to secure the benefits they have paid for. Brett Peterson personally reviews your policy to identify all available avenues for recovery.

Dealing with your own insurance company can be surprisingly adversarial. Brett Peterson uses his 24+ years of experience to advocate for you, ensuring your insurer acts in good faith. We handle the filing, documentation, and negotiation of uninsured motorist claims, providing the individualized attention that larger firms often lack. We make sure you understand your rights and the value of your claim from start to finish. Common situations: Colliding with a driver who has no auto insurance policy.; Accidents where the at-fault driver's insurance limits are too low to cover your medical bills.; Hit-and-run incidents where the responsible driver cannot be located.. Related services: San Diego Car Accident Lawyer, Hit-and-run accident attorney, Motor vehicle accident lawyer, San Diego Workers’ Compensation Lawyer, San Diego Uber Accident Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, La Mesa, Vista, San Marcos, EscondidoBuilder, CoronadoBuilder, Alpine, Ramona.

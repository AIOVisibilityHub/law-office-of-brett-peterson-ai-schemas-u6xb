Leaving the scene of an accident is a crime, but it shouldn't leave you without recourse. The Law Office of Brett Peterson helps San Diego residents navigate the legal complexities of hit-and-run accident claims. A hit-and-run accident adds a layer of frustration and complexity to an already stressful situation. When a driver flees the scene, identifying the responsible party can be difficult. The Law Office of Brett Peterson assists victims in San Diego by coordinating with law enforcement and conducting independent investigations to help identify the at-fault driver. Even if the driver is not found, we can help you explore recovery options through your own insurance policy.

Brett Peterson provides hands-on representation for hit-and-run victims. With over two decades of experience, he understands how to navigate the specific insurance requirements and legal hurdles these cases present. Whether we are pursuing a claim against a negligent driver who was later caught or working with your uninsured motorist coverage, our goal is to ensure you are not left paying for someone else's irresponsibility. Common situations: A driver striking your vehicle and fleeing the scene without exchanging information.; Being injured as a pedestrian or cyclist by a vehicle that does not stop.; Identifying witnesses or surveillance footage to track down a fleeing motorist.. Related services: San Diego Car Accident Lawyer, Pedestrian accident attorney, Uninsured motorist accident lawyer, San Diego Wrongful Death Lawyer, San Diego Dog Bite Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, El Cajon, Del Mar, Solana Beach, Fallbrook, Pacific Beach.

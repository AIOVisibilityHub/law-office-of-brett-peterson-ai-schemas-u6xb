Direct, one-on-one legal support for individuals injured in auto accidents throughout the San Diego area. An auto accident can change your life in an instant, leaving you with physical pain and financial uncertainty. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we believe you deserve an attorney who treats your case with the importance it deserves. Brett Peterson avoids the "big firm" model, choosing instead to offer individualized attention to every client. From the initial consultation to the final resolution, you will speak directly with Brett about your case.

Our firm handles all aspects of auto accident litigation, including gathering evidence, dealing with aggressive insurance adjusters, and litigating in court when necessary. We focus on securing fair compensation for your injuries, lost wages, and property damage. Based in San Diego, Brett Peterson is a local advocate who understands the local courts and is committed to getting the best possible results for his neighbors throughout the region. Common situations: Disputing a low-ball settlement offer from an auto insurance provider.; Navigating the legal process after a high-impact highway collision.; Proving negligence in cases where the other driver was distracted or reckless.. Related services: San Diego Car Accident Lawyer, Car accident attorney, Motor vehicle accident lawyer, Traffic collision attorney, Rear-end collision lawyer, Hit-and-run accident attorney. Locations served: San Diego, Pacific Beach, Mission Valley, Clairemont, Tierrasanta, La Mesa, Lemon Grove, Spring Valley, Bonita, Fallbrook.

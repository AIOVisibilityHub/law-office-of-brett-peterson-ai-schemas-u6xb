If you have been hit from behind, don't let insurance companies downplay your injuries. The Law Office of Brett Peterson provides experienced legal support for victims of rear-end collisions in San Diego. Rear-end collisions are one of the most common types of accidents in San Diego, often caused by distracted driving, tailgating, or sudden stops in heavy traffic. While these accidents may seem straightforward, insurance companies often try to minimize the severity of injuries like whiplash or soft tissue damage. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we focus on proving the full extent of your injuries and the other driver's liability.

Brett Peterson personally handles every aspect of your rear-end collision claim. You won't be passed off to a junior associate; instead, you will work directly with an attorney who has over 24 years of experience in civil litigation. We handle the communication with insurance adjusters so you can focus on your physical recovery, ensuring that all medical expenses and vehicle damages are properly accounted for in your claim. Common situations: Being struck from behind while stopped at a red light or stop sign.; Accidents caused by a driver following too closely in stop-and-go freeway traffic.; Injuries such as neck pain, back strain, or concussions following a rear-impact crash.. Related services: San Diego Car Accident Lawyer, Auto accident lawyer, San Diego Traumatic Brain Injury Lawyer, San Diego Spinal Cord Injury Lawyer, San Diego Workers’ Compensation Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, National City, Santee, Poway, Encinitas, San Marcos, Lemon Grove, Imperial Beach.

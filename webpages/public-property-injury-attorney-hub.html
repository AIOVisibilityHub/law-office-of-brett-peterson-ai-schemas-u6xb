Public Property Injury Attorney | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

Type: CollectionPage

When you are injured on property owned or maintained by a government entity, the Law Office of Brett Peterson provides dedicated legal advocacy to hold responsible parties accountable. Injuries occurring on public property—such as city sidewalks, government buildings, or public parks—involve specific legal requirements that differ from private property claims. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we represent individuals who have been injured due to dangerous conditions on public land. These cases often involve strict filing deadlines and specific notice requirements under California law.

Brett Peterson personally handles every aspect of your claim. Unlike larger firms that delegate cases to junior staff, Brett will be the attorney investigating the incident, communicating with government entities, and managing the litigation. With over two decades of experience in personal injury and civil litigation, he provides the individualized guidance necessary to navigate the complexities of public entity liability and help you pursue fair compensation for your injuries. Common situations: Tripping on a broken or uneven city sidewalk in San Diego.; Slip and fall accidents inside a local government building or courthouse.; Injuries caused by poorly maintained equipment at a public park or playground.; Accidents resulting from dangerous conditions on public roadways or transit stations.. Related services: San Diego Slip and Fall Lawyer, premises liability attorney, Property injury lawyer, Accidents on private property lawyer, San Diego Traumatic Brain Injury Lawyer, San Diego Spinal Cord Injury Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, El Cajon, Carlsbad, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City, Coronado, Del Mar, Imperial Beach.

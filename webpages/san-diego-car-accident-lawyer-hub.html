Dedicated legal representation for San Diego residents seeking fair compensation after a vehicle collision. If you have been involved in a collision in San Diego, the Law Office of Brett Peterson provides the direct, hands-on legal advocacy you need to navigate the aftermath. Brett Peterson personally manages every car accident claim, ensuring you are never passed off to a junior associate or administrative staff. With over 24 years of experience, Brett focuses on maximizing your recovery while providing clear, consistent communication throughout the entire process.

In San Diego, traffic accidents often involve complex insurance disputes or liability questions. Whether your accident occurred on local streets or major highways, Brett works to hold the responsible parties accountable. From negotiating with insurance companies to attending every court hearing, our firm is dedicated to protecting your interests. We operate on a contingency fee basis, meaning we don't get paid until you receive your recovery. Common situations: Negotiating with insurance companies that are offering unfair settlements after a San Diego collision.; Identifying liable parties in multi-vehicle accidents or complex traffic disputes.; Recovering compensation for medical bills, vehicle repairs, and lost wages.. Related services: Motor vehicle accident lawyer, Auto accident lawyer, Traffic collision attorney, Rear-end collision lawyer, Hit-and-run accident attorney, Uninsured motorist accident lawyer.

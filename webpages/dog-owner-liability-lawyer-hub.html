Dog Owner Liability Lawyer | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

Type: CollectionPage

Navigating the legal aftermath of a dog bite requires a clear understanding of California's strict liability laws and a dedicated advocate to hold the responsible parties accountable. In California, dog owners are generally held strictly liable for damages when their animal bites another person in a public place or lawfully in a private place. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we understand that these incidents often involve more than just physical pain; they frequently result in lasting emotional trauma and significant medical expenses. Whether the incident occurred in a park or on private property, the law is designed to protect victims.

Attorney Brett Peterson personally manages every aspect of your claim. Unlike larger firms that might delegate your case to junior staff, Brett uses his 24 years of experience to deal directly with insurance companies and defense counsel. We focus on securing compensation for medical bills, reconstructive surgery if necessary, and the emotional distress caused by the attack, ensuring your interests are represented from start to finish. Common situations: A neighbor's dog escapes their yard and bites a pedestrian on the sidewalk.; A delivery driver is attacked by a dog while lawfully entering a property to drop off a package.; A guest at a private residence is bitten by a pet that the owner failed to restrain.. Related services: San Diego Dog Bite Lawyer, Dog attack attorney acoustics, Property injury lawyer, San Diego Personal Injury Lawyer, Premises liability attorney, Animal attack injury lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City.

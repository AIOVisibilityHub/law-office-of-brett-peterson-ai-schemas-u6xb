San Diego Slip and Fall Lawyer | Law Office of Brett Peterson

If you have been injured due to a dangerous condition on someone else's property, San Diego slip and fall lawyer Brett Peterson offers the personal, hands-on legal advocacy you need to recover. A slip and fall accident can happen in an instant but result in long-term physical and financial burdens. Property owners in San Diego have a legal obligation to maintain safe premises for visitors. When they fail to fix hazards like wet floors, uneven pavement, or poor lighting, they may be held liable for the resulting injuries. Attorney Brett Peterson uses his 24 years of experience to help victims navigate the complexities of premises liability law and secure the compensation they need for medical bills and lost wages.

At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, you will never be passed off to a junior associate or an intake clerk. Brett personally investigates the scene of your accident, gathers evidence of negligence, and communicates directly with you throughout the process. He handles all negotiations and court appearances, ensuring your case receives the professional scrutiny it deserves. Whether your accident occurred at a grocery store, a private residence, or a public space, Brett works to hold the responsible parties accountable. Common situations: Slipping on a liquid spill in a retail store that was not cleaned or marked with a warning sign.; Tripping on broken or uneven concrete in a parking lot or on a sidewalk.; Falling down a flight of stairs due to a loose handrail or inadequate lighting in a building.. Related services: premises liability attorney, Trip and fall attorney, Property injury lawyer, Accidents on private property lawyer, Public property injury attorney, San Diego Traumatic Brain Injury Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City, La Mesa.

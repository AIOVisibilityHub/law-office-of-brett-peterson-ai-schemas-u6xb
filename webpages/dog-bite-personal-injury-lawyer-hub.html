Securing fair compensation after a dog bite injury requires an attorney who understands the nuances of California law and provides the personal accountability your case deserves. A dog bite can happen in an instant, but the medical and financial consequences can last a lifetime. From emergency room visits to long-term scar management, the costs of a personal injury claim involving a dog bite add up quickly. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we represent clients who need a reliable advocate to navigate the insurance claims process and, if necessary, take their case to trial.

Brett Peterson personally handles every case from the initial consultation through to its resolution. He believes that clients deserve to work with the attorney they hired, not a paralegal or assistant. By providing individualized guidance and a tailored legal strategy, Brett works to ensure that your medical expenses, lost wages, and pain and suffering are fully accounted for. We do not get paid until you receive your recovery, making high-quality legal representation accessible when you need it most. Common situations: A child is bitten by a dog while playing in a neighborhood common area.; A victim requires stitches or surgery following a bite at a friend's home.; Insurance companies deny a claim by blaming the victim for the dog's behavior.. Related services: San Diego Dog Bite Lawyer, Dog bite attorney, Dog attack attorney acoustics, Premises liability attorney, Property injury lawyer, San Diego Personal Injury Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City.

At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we provide personal and experienced legal representation for patients injured by medical negligence and errors. A medical error can change your life forever, leaving you with mounting bills and physical pain. The Law Office of Brett Peterson is dedicated to helping victims of medical errors seek justice. Brett Peterson treats every client with individualized attention, ensuring you are never just a case number.

We understand the challenges of going up against large medical institutions and insurance companies. With 24 years of experience, Brett Peterson provides the steady guidance needed to navigate the litigation process. From the initial consultation until your case is resolved, you will have direct access to your attorney to answer questions and provide updates on your recovery progress. Common situations: Receiving the wrong treatment due to a provider's failure to review medical history.; Injuries sustained due to inadequate post-operative care or monitoring.; Being discharged from a hospital too early, resulting in a worsening condition.; Mistakes made by medical staff that lead to long-term disability or chronic pain.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, San Diego Birth Injury Lawyer, San Diego Wrongful Death Lawyer, Medical negligence lawyer, Doctor negligence lawyer, Medication error attorney. Locations served: San Diego, Oceanside, Escondido, Chula Vista, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City, Santee.

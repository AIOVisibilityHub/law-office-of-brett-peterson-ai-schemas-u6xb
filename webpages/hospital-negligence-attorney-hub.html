Hospital Negligence Attorney | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

If you or a loved one has suffered due to inadequate care at a medical facility, the Law Office of Brett Peterson provides direct legal representation for hospital negligence claims throughout San Diego. Medical professionals at hospitals have a duty to provide a standard of care that ensures patient safety. When a hospital’s administrative errors, lack of hygiene, or staffing failures result in serious injury, you need an attorney who understands the complexities of medical litigation. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we investigate hospital protocols and records to identify where the negligence occurred.

Brett Peterson personally manages every aspect of your hospital negligence claim. Unlike large firms that might delegate your case to junior staff, Brett applies over 24 years of experience to your specific situation. From the initial consultation through the final resolution, he ensures your case is handled with the attention and accountability required to hold large medical institutions responsible for their actions. Common situations: Suffering from a severe infection acquired due to unsanitary hospital conditions.; Injuries resulting from improper patient monitoring or nursing staff errors.; Harm caused by hospital administrative failures or equipment malfunctions.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, Medical negligence lawyer, Surgical error lawyer, Doctor negligence lawyer, San Diego Wrongful Death Lawyer, San Diego Birth Injury Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, El Cajon, Escondido, Oceanside, Carlsbad.

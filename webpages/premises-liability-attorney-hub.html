Property owners have a duty to keep their premises safe. When negligence leads to an injury, the Law Office of Brett Peterson provides experienced premises liability representation for residents across San Diego. Premises liability law involves holding property owners and managers responsible for injuries that occur due to unsafe conditions on their land or inside their buildings. These cases require a thorough investigation into whether the owner knew—or should have known—about the hazard and failed to take action. Attorney Brett Peterson has over two decades of experience handling civil litigation and personal injury claims, providing clients with the expertise needed to challenge insurance companies and property owners.

When you hire Brett Peterson, you are hiring a dedicated advocate who will be involved in every detail of your claim. Brett does not use "support staff" to manage his clients; he takes your calls, responds to your emails, and meets with you personally to explain the legal process. From documenting hazardous conditions to litigating your case in court, he ensures that your interests are protected and that you are positioned to achieve the best possible recovery for your injuries. Common situations: Being injured by falling merchandise or debris in a commercial warehouse or store.; Suffering an injury due to a lack of adequate security in a dangerous area.; An accident caused by a hidden defect in a rental property or private home..

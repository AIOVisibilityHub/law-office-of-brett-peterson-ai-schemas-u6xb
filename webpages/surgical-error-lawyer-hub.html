Surgical Error Lawyer | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

Surgical mistakes can lead to permanent injury or prolonged recovery. The Law Office of Brett Peterson represents individuals in San Diego who have been harmed by preventable errors in the operating room. A surgical procedure is a high-stakes event that requires precision and adherence to strict medical standards. When a surgeon or surgical team makes a preventable mistake, the consequences can be life-altering. The Law Office of Brett Peterson helps victims of surgical errors seek compensation for additional medical bills, lost wages, and physical suffering caused by professional negligence in the operating room.

Brett Peterson brings over two decades of civil litigation experience to your case. He handles the investigation and negotiation personally, ensuring that your legal strategy is not lost in a sea of paralegals. Whether the error involved the wrong site, anesthesia complications, or retained instruments, Brett works directly with you to build a case aimed at maximizing your recovery and protecting your future. Common situations: Undergoing a procedure on the wrong body part or the wrong patient.; Suffering complications because a foreign object was left inside the body after surgery.; Experiencing severe injury due to anesthesia errors or nerve damage during a procedure.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, Hospital negligence attorney, Doctor negligence lawyer, San Diego Traumatic Brain Injury Lawyer, San Diego Spinal Cord Injury Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, La Jolla, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City.

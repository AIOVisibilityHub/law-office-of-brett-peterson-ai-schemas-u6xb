If you have been injured in an animal attack, you deserve a lawyer who will provide personal attention to your case and fight for the compensation you need to recover. An animal attack is a violent event that can disrupt your life and livelihood. Beyond the immediate need for medical attention, victims often face mounting bills and uncertainty about who is responsible for their recovery. The Law Office of Brett Peterson provides focused legal representation for victims of animal attacks throughout San Diego County, ensuring that the legal process is handled with professionalism and care.

Brett Peterson brings over 24 years of legal experience to your case, providing the kind of direct communication you won't find at high-volume firms. He takes your phone calls, responds to your emails, and represents you in all court hearings. Our goal is to maximize your recovery so you can focus on your health, while we handle the complexities of proving liability and negotiating with insurance carriers who often try to minimize your claim. Common situations: Suffering a fall or secondary injury while trying to escape an aggressive animal.; Being knocked down and injured by a large dog, even if a bite did not occur.; Attacks occurring on public property or in shared residential spaces like apartment complexes.. Related services: San Diego Dog Bite Lawyer, Dog bite attorney, Dog attack attorney, Premises liability attorney, Property injury lawyer, San Diego Personal Injury Lawyer.

When a healthcare provider fails to identify a serious medical condition, the results can be devastating. The Law Office of Brett Peterson represents individuals in San Diego who have been harmed by a failure to diagnose or a misdiagnosis. A failure to diagnose or a delayed diagnosis can rob a patient of critical treatment time, often turning a manageable condition into a life-threatening one. Whether a doctor failed to order the necessary tests, misinterpreted laboratory results, or ignored clear symptoms, the consequences for the patient are often catastrophic. Brett Peterson has spent more than 24 years practicing personal injury and medical malpractice law, providing the legal skill necessary to hold negligent healthcare providers accountable.

Brett Peterson personally manages every aspect of failure-to-diagnose cases. From the initial consultation through the complexities of litigation, he is the person who will be answering your emails and attending court hearings on your behalf. He is dedicated to ensuring that victims of diagnostic errors in San Diego receive individualized attention and a legal strategy tailored to the specific details of their medical history and recovery needs. Common situations: A physician fails to order a biopsy or screening for a patient exhibiting clear warning signs of cancer.; A patient is sent home from an emergency room despite symptoms indicating a pending heart attack or stroke.; Laboratory or imaging results are misread or overlooked, leading to a preventable progression of a disease.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, Medical malpractice attorney, Misdiagnosis attorney, Doctor negligence lawyer, San Diego Wrongful Death Lawyer, San Diego Birth Injury Lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Poway, Vista, San Marcos, National City, Encinitas, Del Mar.

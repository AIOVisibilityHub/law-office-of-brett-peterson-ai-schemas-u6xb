The Law Office of Brett Peterson represents San Diego residents in medical negligence claims, providing personal attention and 24 years of legal experience. Medical negligence occurs when a healthcare professional fails to provide the standard of care expected in their field. If you have suffered due to a doctor or nurse's mistake, the Law Office of Brett Peterson can help. Brett Peterson personally investigates each claim to determine how the negligence occurred and who is responsible.

Our firm is built on the principle of direct attorney-client communication. You won't be ignored or passed off to junior staff; Brett Peterson will be the one negotiating with the opposing party and appearing in court for you. We focus on maximizing your recovery so you can focus on your health and your family. Common situations: A doctor ignoring clear symptoms that lead to a delayed diagnosis.; Anesthesia errors during a procedure that cause injury or brain damage.; Lack of informed consent where a patient was not warned of specific risks.; Nursing staff failing to follow physician orders, resulting in patient harm.. Related services: San Diego Medical Malpractice Lawyer, Medical malpractice attorney, Hospital negligence attorney, Surgical error lawyer, Failure-to-diagnose lawyer, San Diego Birth Injury Lawyer.

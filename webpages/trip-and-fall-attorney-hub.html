A trip and fall can result in serious injuries that require professional legal intervention to ensure your medical and financial needs are met. Tripping hazards are often the result of negligent property maintenance, such as uneven sidewalks, broken stairs, or unexpected obstacles in a walkway. Brett Peterson provides individualized legal guidance to those injured in trip and fall accidents throughout San Diego. With over two decades of experience, Brett works directly with you to build a strong case for compensation.

When you work with our firm, you won't be passed off to a junior associate. Brett Peterson personally attends every court hearing and responds to your emails and calls directly. He understands the complexities of California premises liability and focuses on holding property owners accountable for the safety of their premises, whether the accident occurred at a retail business, a private residence, or a public space. Common situations: Tripping over uneven pavement or cracked sidewalks.; Falling on stairs that are broken, uneven, or missing proper handrails.; Stumbling over debris or equipment left in public walkways.; Injuries caused by unexpected elevation changes in flooring without warning signs.. Related services: San Diego Slip and Fall Lawyer, premises liability attorney, Slip and fall lawyer, Property injury lawyer, Accidents on private property lawyer, Public property injury attorney. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City, La Mesa.

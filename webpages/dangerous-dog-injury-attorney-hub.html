Dangerous Dog Injury Attorney | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

When a dangerous dog causes severe harm, victims need a seasoned attorney who can navigate the complexities of personal injury law and secure the recovery necessary for healing. Attacks by dangerous or aggressive dogs can lead to catastrophic injuries, including deep lacerations, nerve damage, and permanent scarring. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we advocate for individuals who have been seriously harmed by animals with known dangerous propensities or those who have been victims of sudden, unprovoked aggression. Dealing with the owners of such animals and their insurance providers requires a strategic legal approach.

When you work with our firm, you work directly with Brett Peterson. He handles the investigation, reviews the medical evidence, and navigates the litigation process personally. With over two decades of experience in San Diego civil litigation, Brett ensures that the full extent of your injuries—both physical and psychological—is documented and pursued in your claim. We are committed to holding negligent owners accountable for the danger their animals pose to the community. Common situations: An encounter with a known aggressive dog at a local park resulting in serious injury.; A dog with a history of biting is left unrestrained and attacks a passerby.; A victim suffers severe injuries from a large breed dog that the owner could not control.. Related services: San Diego Dog Bite Lawyer, Dog attack attorney, San Diego Personal Injury Lawyer, Premises liability attorney, Property injury lawyer, Dog bite personal injury lawyer. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City.

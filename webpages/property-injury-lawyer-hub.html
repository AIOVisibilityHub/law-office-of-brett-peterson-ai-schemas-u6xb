Property Injury Lawyer | Law Office of Brett Peterson

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-25

Property owners have a duty to maintain safe environments; when they fail, the Law Office of Brett Peterson helps victims seek justice for their injuries. Property injuries encompass a wide range of accidents caused by unsafe conditions on both residential and commercial premises. At the Law Office of Brett Peterson, we represent individuals who have suffered harm due to property owner negligence. Brett Peterson handles every aspect of these civil litigation claims personally, leveraging more than 24 years of experience to maximize your recovery.

We prioritize clear communication and accountability. Brett ensures that you are informed throughout the entire legal process, from the first consultation to the final resolution. By focusing on individualized strategy rather than high-volume intake, our firm provides the dedicated attention necessary to navigate the complexities of property injury laws in San Diego and the surrounding communities. Common situations: Injuries resulting from structural collapses or falling objects.; Accidents involving faulty elevators or escalators.; Harm caused by toxic exposure or dangerous chemicals on a property.; Injuries sustained due to lack of security or broken gates and fences.. Related services: San Diego Slip and Fall Lawyer, premises liability attorney, Slip and fall lawyer, Trip and fall attorney, Accidents on private property lawyer, Public property injury attorney. Locations served: San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, National City, La Mesa.
